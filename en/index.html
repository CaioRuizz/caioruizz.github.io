Focus over time

Live

Market expectations, week by week.

The Central Bank’s Focus survey only shows this week’s market expectations. This site shows how each forecast for inflation, the Selic rate, GDP and the dollar changed since the first projection, compared with what actually happened. The site is in Portuguese.

  • Data: Central Bank expectations API since 2012, updated every business day
  • Charts: median, lowest and highest forecast and the realized value for each year
focus.caioruiz.com →
