Painel ETF

In development

A research platform for ETFs listed on B3.

Brings together prices since 2018, holdings, distributions, costs and data from the Central Bank of Brazil, the National Treasury, CVM and the SEC to answer questions like: how much do two ETFs overlap? Which ETFs hold a given stock? What are the risk, return and cost of each one?

  • Data: scheduled daily ingestion, backups with tested restores and alerting
  • Analytics: overlap by asset, issuer and look-through, Stock Locator, return, risk, correlation, aggregated fundamentals and costs
